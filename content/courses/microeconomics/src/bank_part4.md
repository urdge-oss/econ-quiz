@level 10 | the-few | The Few | Monopolistic competition & oligopoly | 73-80
@brief Monopolistic competition: many firms, differentiated products, free entry. In the long run demand is tangent to LAC: zero profit, but P > MC.
@brief Excess capacity: output stays below the minimum of LAC — the price of product variety.
@brief Cournot: firms choose quantities, each taking the rival's output as given. The Nash equilibrium lies between competition and monopoly.
@brief Bertrand with identical goods: price falls to MC even with two firms. Stackelberg: the firm that commits first wins.
@brief Kinked demand explains sticky prices. A dominant firm prices off residual demand. Cartels need inelastic demand, inelastic outside supply and discipline.
